Alfredo

Ingredients:

  • Fettucine Pasta
  • Freshly, finely shredded parmigiano reggiano
  • Heavy Cream
  • Butter
  • Salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Garlic (optional)

Steps

  1. Boil pasta until al dente
  2. Add butter and parmesan and toss until thoroughly mixed
  3. Add a small amount of heavy cream and continue to toss
  4. Salt, pepper and garlic to taste
